1. Boarding Collection

Driver collects full fare from each passenger at boarding.

2. One-Seat Remittance

Driver remits the value of one seat to the official HVR account before takeoff.

3. Manifest + Proof

Driver sends signed passengers manifest + proof of remittance via WhatsApp.

4. Dispatch Certificate

HVR issues official Dispatch Certificate + unique codes to all passengers and the driver.
