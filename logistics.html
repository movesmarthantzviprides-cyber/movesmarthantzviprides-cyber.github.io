🛡️ Insurance — Luggage, Waybills & Cargo

Passenger accident cover on HVR trips is separate from goods / cargo cover. Protecting the value of items needs Goods-in-Transit (GIT) style arrangements.

Passenger luggage (with rider)

Personal bags travelling with a booked passenger on an active HVR trip — handled under trip rules; declare excess/high-value items.

Unaccompanied luggage / waybill

Items without a passenger — recommend declared value and, for higher sums, GIT or single-journey cover.

Cargo / commercial

Stock and merchandise in transit — typically needs Goods-in-Transit insurance (annual or per journey).

Documents

Low physical value but high importance — focus on chain of custody, handover record, and limited declared-value options.

Option Best for Notes (Nigeria market)
Goods-in-Transit (GIT) Cargo, stock, frequent waybills Covers loss/damage in road transit (accident, theft, loading). Offered by Leadway, AIICO, AXA Mansard, NEM, Custodian and others. Annual or single-journey.
Declared-value add-on One-off high-value luggage/parcel State approx. value at booking; HVR may facilitate referral to insurer — not automatic full replacement.
Carrier vs owner policy Shippers / SMEs Vehicle insurance does not replace cargo cover. Owners of goods should hold GIT for serious commercial loads.
Group accident (trip) Passengers & drivers People cover on active HVR operations — not a substitute for goods cover.
HVR position: Active HVR trips aim for profiled vehicles, tracking and professional handling. For commercial cargo and high-value consignments, clients should arrange or request Goods-in-Transit cover with a licensed insurer. HVR can introduce partners (including discussions with Official Insurer channels) — terms are set by the insurer, not by the website quote. Always declare fragile and high-value items before loading.
